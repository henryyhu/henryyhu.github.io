Yuheng Hu

FCOB Distinguished Associate Professor

I am a faculty member in the Department of Accounting & Management Information Systems at The Ohio State University’s Fisher College of Business. My research examines generative AI, human–AI interaction, and digital platforms.

My work has appeared in leading journals and conferences including Information Systems Research, MIS Quarterly, Journal of Management Information Systems, Production and Operations Management, INFORMS Journal on Computing, ACM Transactions on Management Information Systems, IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering, AAAI, IJCAI, ICWSM, and CHI.

Before joining Ohio State, I was a tenured Associate Professor at the University of Illinois Chicago and a Research Staff Member at IBM Research AI. I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Arizona State University.
